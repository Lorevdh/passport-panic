# ✈️ Passport Panic: Airport Eats

A fast-paced mini-game made for a one-day game jam!  
Serve international travelers the right dish before they lose their patience and storm off.

---

## 🕹️ Gameplay

Travelers from around the world arrive at your airport food court with specific cravings.  
Click the correct food stall (Ramen, Sushi, Onigiri) before their patience bar runs out.

**Scoring:**
- ✅ Correct serve: +100 points
- ❌ Wrong dish: −50 points
- 😡 Too slow: traveler storms off, −50 points

Try to keep everyone happy before time runs out!

---

## 🌍 Countries Featured

This MVP build includes dishes from:

- 🇯🇵 **Japan** — Ramen, Sushi, Onigiri  
- 🇮🇹 **Italy** and 🇧🇷 **Brazil** referenced visually/textually for international flavor
